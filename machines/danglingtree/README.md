## General takeaways

This lab reinforced a few habits I want to carry into future assessments:

- Verify each result before building the next step on top of it.
- Keep credentials and authentication artifacts out of public notes and repositories.
- Record which environment or machine instance each result belongs to.
- Treat errors as evidence to investigate, not as proof that the whole approach has failed.
- Keep a clear distinction between confirmed access and assumptions about permissions.
